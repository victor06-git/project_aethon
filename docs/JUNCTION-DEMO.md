# Revi at Junction: two submissions, one demo

**Shared case:** A first-time car buyer in Finland wants a used Toyota Yaris Hybrid for €8,000 within 12 months. The buyer's banking history is a synthetic S-Pankki challenge customer. The car listings and published conditions should come from the live web when research is enabled. Treat listings as leads to check with sellers, since inventory and prices can change.

Use the latest build based on [`test`](https://github.com/victor06-git/project_aethon/tree/test). Start each pitch in a fresh browser session at phone width or append `?embed`. Show the connection and data labels before the first question. For a local rehearsal, `npm ci && npm start` runs the full application. Run with `REVI_DATA_SOURCE=csv` and `REVI_DATASET` pointing to the official transaction file for the S-Bank case. The standalone HTML and the fixture server are useful offline fallbacks; neither is proof of live research or access to sponsor rows.

## S-Bank submission: Personal Economic Trainer

**Submission copy:** Revi helps young adults understand where their money goes and test the effect of a real goal. It explains mandatory regular bills, mandatory irregular costs, and everyday spending from the supplied synthetic S-Pankki transactions. Ask about a purchase, confirm the brief, and see how much to set aside each month, what stays in reserve, and what it costs to own afterwards. Change the date or an assumption and see what moved. The user can save a feasible plan; Revi then accounts for that commitment in its spending estimate. The mobile prototype shows how this could fit into S-mobiili with the customer in control.

**Live walkthrough (about 90 seconds):**

1. Open the coach at phone width. Show the synthetic data label and the transaction period. Ask “Where did my money go?” Open the baskets and explain why a recurring bill and an irregular repair affect spending room differently. On the default challenge customer, a second prompt is “How much did I spend on dining out last month?”
2. Ask “Could I buy a used Toyota Yaris Hybrid for €8,000 in 12 months?” Confirm the brief. Show how the calculation separates the cash needed upfront, monthly savings capacity, the buffer, and the running costs. Keep external assumptions visibly labelled; this demo also works with a different price typed by the judge.
3. Ask “What if I wait 3 more months?” Point to the change from €666.67 to €533.33/month and the remaining gap. For a feasible alternative on the same profile, use €6,000, 24 months and 15% less everyday spending. That requires €250/month against €414.98 in projected monthly room, including money that may be redirected from existing investment contributions. Save it and show the prepared transfer or the set-aside in safe-to-spend.

**Credible S-mobiili integration:** Use a native screen or authenticated webview. An S-Bank backend would issue a short-lived session tied to the signed-in customer, then supply authorized account snapshots, ledger categories and timestamps, recent transaction history and known recurring commitments. The calculation service would return dated, explainable aggregates, scenarios and plan proposals. An authenticated bank service would own persistence and consent. A native banking action could prefill a savings instruction with the amount and date, and require the customer's usual final confirmation. This prototype uses a fixed synthetic customer, keeps plan state in the browser, and neither connects to a bank login nor moves money. The optional other-bank account is a mock of PSD2 access.

**Data verification:** The official Git LFS file has 3,626,755 transactions for 5,100 synthetic customers, covering 1 October 2024 to 30 September 2026. The default CUST00001 has 581 transactions and one account. An independent calculation reconciled the account balances and returned €115.43 across 10 dining payments in August 2026. Across the last 365 days, monthly income averaged €2,335.02, with €640.30 in regular commitments, €358.13 reserved for irregular costs and €1,084.25 in everyday spending. The result is €252.34/month before the existing €231.50/month of investment contributions. Salary sweeps are reserved cash outflows rather than consumption. The exported data contains no refund rows; net refunds and two-account behavior are covered by regression fixtures.

## Exa submission: The Internet Butler

**Submission copy:** Revi does the legwork for first-time used-car buyers in Finland. It turns a conversation into a confirmed brief, uses Exa to find current options and the costs hidden behind the sticker price, and ties usable prices and conditions to the passages that support them. Pick a sourced option and see how it changes what you need upfront, what you would save, and what ownership could cost each month. Revi prepares a savings checklist for the option you choose. You decide whether to agree to it and what the app should recheck.

**Live walkthrough (about 90 seconds):**

1. In a fresh session, show live research is configured. Use the first-car prompt, confirm the brief, and show the plan before research. Ask Revi to research the goal with Exa. Open at least one listing and a cost excerpt. Show the stated price, date if the publisher gives one, conditions if available, and any line still based on an assumption.
2. Pick a sourced option. Point to the change in the savings amount and separate that calculation from the sourced price. Open the prepared seller inquiry: it asks for availability, the total cash price, history and a test drive. No banking data goes into it, and the user decides whether to send it. Have a judge name another model (for example, “Switch to a used Skoda Fabia”). Confirm the revised brief and run research again. Show the new query and sources. If a price or condition is missing, leave the line as an assumption and let the judge correct it.
3. Select a feasible option or timing, then save the plan explicitly. Open the prepared checklist and show the user-controlled next step. Optionally approve a price recheck and reopen the plan. The current prototype rechecks on demand or on opening the plan with monitoring enabled. A verified market change can become a proposal; accepting it is a separate action. The prototype has no background worker or notifications.
